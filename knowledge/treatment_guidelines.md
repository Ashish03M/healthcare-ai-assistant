# Treatment Guidelines and Decision Rules (India)

## Severity Levels

### SELF_CARE
Patient can manage at home with over-the-counter medicines available at any Indian pharmacy (medical store). No doctor visit needed unless symptoms worsen or persist.

### APPOINTMENT
Patient should schedule a doctor appointment at a hospital or clinic. Not immediately life-threatening but needs professional evaluation and possibly prescription treatment.

### URGENT
Patient should visit the nearest hospital emergency or urgent care immediately. Condition needs prompt attention.

### EMERGENCY
Patient should call 108 (National Emergency Number) or 102 (Ambulance) or rush to the nearest hospital emergency department. Potentially life-threatening condition requiring immediate medical intervention.

## When to Recommend SELF_CARE

### Headache (Tension Type)
- **OTC Medicine**: Paracetamol (Crocin/Dolo 650) 500-650mg every 4-6 hours, or Ibuprofen (Brufen) 200-400mg every 6-8 hours
- **Self-Care Steps**: Rest in a quiet dark room, stay hydrated (drink nimbu paani/ORS), apply Amrutanjan or Zandu Balm on forehead, gentle neck stretches
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Headaches occur >15 days/month, OTC medicines don't help, new type of headache, age >50 with new headaches

### Common Cold / Viral Fever
- **OTC Medicine**: Paracetamol (Crocin/Dolo 650) for fever and body aches, Cetirizine (Alerid/Cetzine) 10mg for runny nose, Sinarest or Vicks Action 500 for congestion, Strepsils or Cofsils for sore throat
- **Self-Care Steps**: Rest, drink warm fluids (haldi doodh/turmeric milk, ginger tea, kadha), steam inhalation with Vicks VapoRub, gargle with warm salt water, eat light khichdi or dal chawal
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Symptoms last >10 days, fever >103°F (39.4°C), difficulty breathing, severe body aches, dengue-like symptoms (high fever with body pain and rash)

### Allergies (Seasonal/Environmental)
- **OTC Medicine**: Cetirizine (Alerid/Cetzine) 10mg or Levocetirizine (Levocet) 5mg daily, Nasivion nasal drops for congestion, Patanol eye drops for itchy eyes
- **Self-Care Steps**: Minimize exposure to dust and pollution (common in Indian cities), wear a mask during high pollution days, keep windows closed, use an air purifier if possible
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: OTC medicines insufficient, symptoms affect daily life, wheezing or asthma-like symptoms

### Mild Stomach Issues (Gastroenteritis / Loose Motions)
- **OTC Medicine**: ORS (Electral/Enerzal) for rehydration, Loperamide (Eldoper/Imodium) for diarrhea, Ondem (Ondansetron) 4mg for nausea, Pudin Hara or Hajmola for mild indigestion
- **Self-Care Steps**: Drink ORS or nimbu paani with salt and sugar, eat light food (khichdi, curd rice, banana), avoid spicy and oily food, rest, wash hands frequently
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Symptoms >3 days, blood in stool, fever >104°F, signs of dehydration (dark urine, dizziness), severe abdominal cramping

### Acid Reflux / Acidity
- **OTC Medicine**: Gelusil MPS or Digene for immediate relief, Pantoprazole (Pan 40) or Omeprazole (Omez) 20mg before breakfast, Rantac (Ranitidine) 150mg
- **Self-Care Steps**: Avoid trigger foods (spicy curry, chai on empty stomach, fried food, citrus), eat smaller meals, don't lie down for 2-3 hours after eating, sleep with head elevated
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Symptoms >2 weeks despite OTC treatment, difficulty swallowing, unintentional weight loss

### Minor Skin Rash / Fungal Infection
- **OTC Medicine**: Candid-B cream for fungal infections, Caladryl lotion for itching, Betnovate-C cream (short-term) for inflammation, Cetirizine for itch relief
- **Self-Care Steps**: Keep affected area clean and dry (especially important in humid Indian climate), wear loose cotton clothes, avoid scratching, use antifungal powder (Candid powder) in skin folds
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Rash spreading, signs of infection (pus, warmth, fever), ringworm not improving after 1 week of cream, blisters

### Mild Lower Back Pain
- **OTC Medicine**: Ibuprofen (Brufen) 400mg every 6-8 hours, or Diclofenac gel (Voveran Emulgel) applied topically, Paracetamol (Dolo 650) for pain
- **Self-Care Steps**: Stay active (gentle walking), apply ice first 48 hours then warm compress, gentle stretching and yoga (Bhujangasana, Marjariasana), good posture, avoid sitting on floor for long
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Pain >2 weeks, numbness/tingling in legs, weakness, pain after injury, bladder/bowel problems

### Conjunctivitis (Aankh Aana / Pink Eye)
- **OTC Medicine**: Artificial tears (Refresh Tears), cool compresses for itching
- **Self-Care Steps**: Wash hands frequently, don't touch/rub eyes, don't share towels or pillows, avoid kajal/eye makeup, clean eyes with clean water
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Severe pain, vision changes, thick discharge, no improvement after 5-7 days

### Mild Sinusitis
- **OTC Medicine**: Sinarest or Nasivion nasal drops for congestion, saline nasal wash (Nasoclear), Paracetamol or Ibuprofen for pain, Mucinex/Ascoril to thin mucus
- **Self-Care Steps**: Steam inhalation 3-4 times a day (add eucalyptus oil), warm compress on face, stay hydrated, sleep with head elevated, avoid AC draft directly on face
- **Escalate to APPOINTMENT if**: Symptoms >10 days, symptoms worsen after initial improvement, high fever, severe headache

## When to Recommend APPOINTMENT

Recommend scheduling a doctor appointment when:
1. Symptoms persist beyond the typical self-care duration
2. OTC treatments from the medical store are not providing relief
3. The condition requires prescription medication (antibiotics, specific treatments)
4. The condition needs professional diagnosis (blood tests, X-ray, MRI at a diagnostic centre)
5. There are moderate red flags that need evaluation
6. Chronic conditions need ongoing management

### Typical Appointment-Level Conditions:
- Dengue/Malaria suspected (fever with body pain, especially during monsoon season)
- Strep throat or persistent sore throat (needs antibiotic)
- UTI (needs urine test and antibiotics)
- Persistent back pain with sciatica
- Chronic skin conditions (eczema, psoriasis flares)
- Asthma exacerbations
- Suspected high blood pressure or diabetes
- Persistent anxiety or depression
- Sprains and musculoskeletal injuries
- Migraines (for preventive treatment plan)
- Typhoid suspected (prolonged fever)
- Tuberculosis screening (persistent cough >2 weeks)

## When to Advise EMERGENCY (Call 108)

Immediately advise the patient to call 108 (National Emergency Number) or rush to the nearest hospital for:

1. **Chest Pain**: Crushing/squeezing pain, especially with radiation to arm/jaw, shortness of breath, sweating, nausea — rush to nearest hospital (Fortis, Apollo, AIIMS, Max)
2. **Stroke Signs**: Sudden facial drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty (remember FAST: Face, Arms, Speech, Time)
3. **Severe Breathing Difficulty**: Cannot speak in full sentences, blue lips/fingertips, severe wheezing unresponsive to inhaler
4. **Severe Allergic Reaction (Anaphylaxis)**: Throat swelling, difficulty breathing after food/insect sting, widespread hives
5. **Head Injury**: Loss of consciousness, confusion, repeated vomiting, seizures
6. **Severe Bleeding**: Uncontrollable bleeding, deep wounds — apply pressure and rush to hospital
7. **Suicidal Thoughts**: Active suicidal ideation — contact Vandrevala Foundation helpline 1860-2662-345 or iCall 9152987821
8. **High Fever with Stiff Neck**: Possible meningitis — rush to hospital
9. **Severe Abdominal Pain**: Sudden onset, rigid abdomen, fever — possible appendicitis
10. **Snake Bite / Animal Bite**: Rush to nearest government hospital for anti-venom/rabies treatment
11. **Dengue Warning Signs**: Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, rapid breathing, bleeding gums, blood in vomit — rush to hospital
12. **Heat Stroke**: In summer (loo season), if body temperature >104°F with confusion and no sweating — call 108 immediately
