# Medical Symptoms and Conditions Reference (India)

## 1. Common Cold
**Symptoms**: Runny nose, sneezing, mild sore throat, low-grade fever (below 100.4°F/38°C), mild body aches, nasal congestion
**Duration**: 7-10 days
**Severity**: SELF_CARE
**Red Flags**: None typical
**Notes**: Very common during weather changes (monsoon, winter). Viral infection, antibiotics not effective.

## 2. Viral Fever
**Symptoms**: High fever (102-104°F/39-40°C), severe body aches, extreme fatigue, headache, chills, sore throat, sometimes with loose motions
**Duration**: 3-7 days typically
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Fever >5 days, rash with fever (rule out dengue), severe joint pain, bleeding from gums/nose
**Specialty**: General Practice
**Notes**: Very common in India especially during monsoon. Must rule out dengue, malaria, chikungunya, and typhoid through blood tests.

## 3. Dengue Fever
**Symptoms**: High fever (104°F/40°C), severe headache behind eyes, severe joint and muscle pain ("breakbone fever"), skin rash, nausea, vomiting, mild bleeding (nose/gums)
**Duration**: 2-7 days fever, recovery 1-2 weeks
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT (mild) / EMERGENCY (severe with warning signs)
**Red Flags**: Severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, rapid breathing, bleeding gums, blood in vomit, fatigue, restlessness — these are DENGUE WARNING SIGNS requiring EMERGENCY care
**Specialty**: General Practice (mild), Emergency (severe)
**Notes**: Spread by Aedes mosquito. Very common during and after monsoon season (July-November). No specific medicine — supportive care, hydration, monitor platelet count. NEVER take Aspirin or Ibuprofen with suspected dengue.

## 4. Tension Headache
**Symptoms**: Dull pressing pain on both sides of head, tightness around forehead, mild sensitivity to light or noise
**Duration**: 30 minutes to several hours
**Severity**: SELF_CARE
**Red Flags**: Sudden severe headache ("thunderclap"), headache after head injury, fever with stiff neck
**Notes**: Often triggered by stress, poor posture, dehydration, excessive screen time, or skipping meals

## 5. Migraine
**Symptoms**: Intense throbbing pain usually on one side, nausea, vomiting, extreme sensitivity to light and sound, visual aura (flashing lights, blind spots)
**Duration**: 4-72 hours
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: First-ever severe headache, headache with fever and stiff neck, headache after head trauma
**Specialty**: Neurology
**Notes**: May be preceded by aura. Common triggers: stress, irregular sleep, certain foods, weather changes, bright light

## 6. Typhoid Fever
**Symptoms**: Prolonged high fever (rising stepwise over days), headache, weakness, stomach pain, constipation or diarrhea, loss of appetite, coated tongue
**Duration**: Requires treatment (antibiotics for 7-14 days)
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Very high persistent fever, severe abdominal pain, confusion, intestinal bleeding
**Specialty**: General Practice
**Notes**: Caused by contaminated food/water. Common in India. Diagnosed via Widal test or blood culture. Requires antibiotics.

## 7. Gastroenteritis (Stomach Flu / Loose Motions)
**Symptoms**: Nausea, vomiting, watery diarrhea, abdominal cramps, low-grade fever, muscle aches
**Duration**: 1-3 days
**Severity**: SELF_CARE
**Red Flags**: Blood in stool, high fever (>104°F), signs of dehydration (dark urine, dizziness, dry mouth), symptoms lasting >3 days
**Notes**: Very common, especially during monsoon. Focus on ORS and hydration. Often caused by contaminated food or water.

## 8. Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)
**Symptoms**: Burning sensation when urinating, frequent urge to urinate, cloudy or strong-smelling urine, pelvic pain, lower abdominal discomfort
**Duration**: Requires treatment
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Fever, back pain (flank pain), blood in urine (may indicate kidney infection)
**Specialty**: General Practice
**Notes**: More common in women. Drink plenty of water. Requires urine test and antibiotics.

## 9. Lower Back Pain (Mechanical)
**Symptoms**: Dull aching pain in lower back, stiffness, limited range of motion, pain worsens with movement or prolonged sitting
**Duration**: Days to weeks
**Severity**: SELF_CARE
**Red Flags**: Numbness in legs, loss of bladder/bowel control, pain after trauma, unexplained weight loss
**Notes**: Common due to long sitting hours (IT professionals), poor posture, sitting on floor. Yoga and gentle exercise help.

## 10. Sciatica
**Symptoms**: Sharp shooting pain from lower back down one leg, numbness or tingling in leg/foot, weakness in affected leg, pain worse when sitting
**Duration**: Weeks to months
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Progressive weakness, loss of bladder/bowel control, pain after severe trauma
**Specialty**: Orthopedics
**Notes**: Caused by compression of sciatic nerve. Very common in people with desk jobs.

## 11. Skin Rash / Fungal Infection
**Symptoms**: Red itchy rash, ring-shaped patches (ringworm), bumps or blisters, dry cracked skin, burning or stinging, white patches in skin folds
**Duration**: 2-4 weeks with treatment
**Severity**: SELF_CARE
**Red Flags**: Widespread rash with fever, blisters on face/genitals, signs of infection (pus)
**Notes**: Very common in India's hot humid climate, especially during monsoon. Fungal infections (daad/ringworm) common in groin, underarms, between toes. Keep skin dry.

## 12. Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)
**Symptoms**: Dry itchy skin, red to brownish patches, small raised bumps that may leak fluid, thickened cracked skin
**Duration**: Chronic, recurring
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Signs of skin infection, severe discomfort affecting sleep, widespread flare
**Specialty**: Dermatology
**Notes**: Can worsen in winter (dry air) or monsoon (humidity). Common in people with allergies or asthma.

## 13. Acid Reflux / Acidity (GERD)
**Symptoms**: Heartburn (chest burning after eating), regurgitation of food or sour liquid, difficulty swallowing, feeling of lump in throat, sour taste in mouth
**Duration**: Recurring
**Severity**: SELF_CARE
**Red Flags**: Difficulty swallowing worsening over time, unintentional weight loss, chest pain with shortness of breath, vomiting blood
**Notes**: Very common in India due to spicy food, irregular eating habits, excessive chai/coffee. Avoid eating late at night.

## 14. Asthma Exacerbation
**Symptoms**: Wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, coughing (especially at night), difficulty speaking in full sentences
**Duration**: Hours to days
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Severe breathlessness at rest, inability to speak, blue lips/fingertips, no relief from rescue inhaler
**Specialty**: Pulmonology
**Notes**: Can be triggered by air pollution (very common in Delhi NCR during winter), dust, cold air, smoke from crop burning. Severe case with blue lips = EMERGENCY (call 108)

## 15. Malaria
**Symptoms**: Cyclical high fever with chills and rigors, sweating after fever subsides, headache, body aches, nausea, vomiting, fatigue
**Duration**: Requires treatment
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Very high fever, confusion, severe anemia, jaundice, dark urine, difficulty breathing
**Specialty**: General Practice
**Notes**: Common in monsoon season. Spread by Anopheles mosquito. Diagnosed via blood smear or rapid test. Requires antimalarial medication. P. falciparum malaria can be life-threatening.

## 16. Conjunctivitis (Aankh Aana / Pink Eye)
**Symptoms**: Redness in one or both eyes, itchiness, gritty feeling, discharge that crusts overnight, tearing
**Duration**: 1-2 weeks
**Severity**: SELF_CARE
**Red Flags**: Severe eye pain, vision changes, sensitivity to light, symptoms not improving after 1 week
**Notes**: Very contagious, common during monsoon. Viral: resolves on its own. Bacterial: may need antibiotic drops from doctor. Avoid touching eyes and sharing towels.

## 17. Sinusitis
**Symptoms**: Facial pain/pressure, nasal congestion, thick yellow/green nasal discharge, reduced sense of smell, headache, postnasal drip, cough
**Duration**: Acute: 2-4 weeks
**Severity**: SELF_CARE (acute) / APPOINTMENT (if >10 days or worsening)
**Red Flags**: Severe headache, high fever, swelling around eyes, vision changes, stiff neck
**Specialty**: ENT
**Notes**: Can worsen during pollution season in Delhi NCR. Steam inhalation is very effective.

## 18. Sprained Ankle
**Symptoms**: Pain especially when bearing weight, swelling, bruising, limited range of motion, popping sensation at time of injury
**Duration**: 2-6 weeks
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Unable to bear any weight, severe deformity, numbness, severe swelling immediately
**Specialty**: Orthopedics
**Notes**: RICE protocol (Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation) for initial care. Get X-ray to rule out fracture.

## 19. Hypertension (High Blood Pressure)
**Symptoms**: Usually asymptomatic ("silent killer"), occasional headaches, shortness of breath, nosebleeds in severe cases
**Duration**: Chronic
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Severe headache with confusion, chest pain, vision problems, difficulty breathing
**Specialty**: Cardiology
**Notes**: Very common in India due to sedentary lifestyle, high salt diet, stress. Regular BP monitoring essential. Available at any nearby pharmacy.

## 20. Anxiety Disorder
**Symptoms**: Excessive worry, restlessness, fatigue, difficulty concentrating, muscle tension, sleep problems, irritability, rapid heartbeat, palpitations
**Duration**: Persistent (>6 months)
**Severity**: APPOINTMENT
**Red Flags**: Suicidal thoughts, panic attacks with chest pain, inability to function daily
**Specialty**: Psychiatry
**Notes**: Increasingly common in Indian urban population. Combination of therapy and medication most effective. Helplines: Vandrevala Foundation 1860-2662-345, iCall 9152987821.
